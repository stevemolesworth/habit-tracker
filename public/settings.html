Settings

Focuses

Focuses are the broad areas of your life you want to improve or pay attention to — things like friends and family, work, home admin, financial management, or staying calm.

At the end of each day, you'll be asked to rate how well you focused on each one, on a scale of 1 to 5.

Deactivating a focus hides it from future check-ins but keeps your historical data intact.

Behaviours

Behaviours are habits or actions you want to track — things you did or didn't do during the day. They can be positive (like reading or getting outside) or negative (like eating chocolate or doom-scrolling).

Each check-in, you simply tick the ones that applied to your day.

When creating a behaviour, give it a label and an impact score to reflect how good or bad it is for you.

Supplements

Supplements are vitamins, minerals, or other health products you take regularly and want to keep track of.

Add the ones that are part of your routine, and each day you'll be asked to confirm whether you took them.
