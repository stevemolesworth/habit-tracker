About

Chicken Check-In is a personal wellbeing and habit tracker built around two short check-ins a day — one in the morning, one in the evening. It takes about two minutes each time.

What is it?

Chicken Check-In helps you build a picture of your own wellbeing over time. Every check-in captures a snapshot — how you slept, how you feel, what you did, what you consumed — alongside real weather data for your location. Over days and weeks, patterns emerge that are genuinely hard to spot in the moment.

The morning check-in focuses on sleep and intent. The end of day check-in focuses on what actually happened — exercise, focus, alcohol, behaviours, reflection. Together they give you a much richer picture than a single daily entry ever could.

Who is it for?

Chicken Check-In is for anyone who wants to understand themselves a little better. You don't need to be chasing peak performance or following a strict programme. You just need to be curious about the connection between how you live and how you feel.

It's particularly useful if you've ever wondered:

  • Does bad weather actually affect my mood?
  • Am I sleeping enough, and does it matter?
  • How does exercise affect my focus the next day?
  • Are there habits I keep meaning to build — but never quite do?

What makes Chicken Check-In different?

Two check-ins, twice the signal

Morning and evening capture fundamentally different things. A single daily entry at 9pm can't tell you how sleep quality affects next-day mood. Chicken Check-In can.

Weather built in

Chicken Check-In captures weather data automatically at the moment of every check-in — temperature, conditions, your location. No other habit tracker does this out of the box. Over time you can genuinely see whether grey days drag your mood down, or whether you run more in the summer.

Your data, your way

Configure your own behaviours and supplements. The things you track should reflect your life, not a generic template. Add what matters to you, remove what doesn't.

Getting started

  1. Set up what you want to track — head straight to Settings before your first check-in. Add your behaviours (the habits you want to monitor) and any supplements you take. These appear as quick checkboxes on every check-in. Be specific: "Read for 20 minutes" is better than "Read more".
  2. Do your first check-in — Chicken Check-In will automatically show you the right check-in for the time of day. Morning before 5pm, evening after. Just fill in what you can and hit submit.
  3. Bookmark the page — see below for how to make Chicken Check-In as frictionless as possible.

Not about perfect streaks

Most habit trackers subtly punish you for missing a day. A broken streak, a gap in the chart. Chicken Check-In doesn't work that way.

The goal is to collect enough data to see patterns — and any data is better than none. Checked in three times this week? That's three data points you didn't have before. Missed a week because life got busy? That's fine. Come back when you can.

You can also add retrospective data. If you forgot to check in yesterday, you can go back via the Calendar and fill it in. Historical weather data is fetched automatically for past dates, so your records stay accurate even when entered late.

Making it frictionless

The single biggest predictor of whether you'll stick with Chicken Check-In is how easy it is to open it. Remove every obstacle you can.

Bookmark it

Add Chicken Check-In to your browser bookmarks and put it somewhere prominent — your bookmarks bar, your browser home page, or a pinned tab.

Set it as your browser home page

In Chrome, Firefox, or Safari: open your browser settings, find the home page option, and set it to chickencheckin.netlify.app. Every time you open a new browser window, Chicken Check-In is right there.

Add to your iPhone or iPad home screen

Chicken Check-In works great as a home screen app on iOS. It opens full screen, like a native app, with no browser chrome in the way.

  1. Open Chicken Check-In in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Give it a name — "Chicken Check-In" works well — then tap Add.

The 🐔 icon will appear on your home screen. Tap it to open Chicken Check-In instantly, just like any other app.

Understanding your trends

The Reports page is where Chicken Check-In starts to pay off. You'll see charts for mood, sleep, focus, and alcohol alongside a visual grid of your daily habits.

Some things to look for:

  • Mood vs weather — do you reliably dip when it's overcast or cold?
  • Sleep vs next-day mood — compare your sleep quality chart with the mood chart. Is there a one-day lag?
  • Exercise vs focus — does working out in the morning change how productive you feel at work?
  • Alcohol vs sleep — even a small amount can fragment sleep. The data often makes this visible in a way that gut feel doesn't.

You're not looking for perfect correlations. You're looking for honest patterns in your own life — ones that might nudge you to make slightly better choices, more often.
