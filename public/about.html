About

Two short check-ins a day — 30 seconds in the morning, one minute in the evening. Over time, patterns emerge.

What you track

Morning — sleep quality, bedtime, wake time, mood, supplements.

Evening — mood, behaviours, alcohol, exercise, mindfulness, project progress. Weather is captured automatically at both check-ins.

Everything is configurable. Add your own mood dimensions, behaviours, supplements, and projects in Settings.

Over time

The Reports page shows charts for mood, sleep, alcohol, and habits. With enough data, patterns emerge — how sleep affects mood, whether exercise helps, what grey days do to your energy.

No streaks, no pressure

Miss a week? Come back when you can. Any data is better than none. You can fill in past dates via the Calendar — historical weather is fetched automatically.

Getting started

  1. Set up Settings first — add the behaviours, moods, and projects you want to track.
  2. Do your first check-in — Chicken Check-In shows the right one for the time of day.
  3. Make it easy to open — bookmark it, or add it to your iPhone home screen (Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen).
